**All 124 cells captured with 5/5 invocations.** Within-cell wall CV is under 1% for almost every cell (the one exception noted below).

> ⚠️ **Allocation counters in this result set are ~8× inflated.** OCaml 5.5.0's `Runtime_events` emits `EV_C_MINOR_ALLOCATED_WORDS` in *bytes*, not words. Measured here: `minor_words / minor_collections = 7.86 × s`, where it should be ≈ `1.0 × s`. So `minor_words`, `promoted_words`, `total_heap_words` and anything derived from them are wrong by ~8× **in this sweep only** — both trunk-based sweeps in `results/latest` test clean at 0.94–0.99×. Wall, RSS, `gc_overhead`, latency and all `perf` counters are unaffected.

## TL;DR

- **`-flambda` is the real win: -8.0% wall geomean, and it pays for it in RSS (+6.8%).** Instruction count drops -8.0% geomean, so this is genuine work removed, not just better scheduling. `-fp-flambda` is statistically the same story (-8.6% wall, +6.8% RSS).
- **`-fp` alone is free on wall time (-0.4% geomean) but costs +6.3% instructions.** Frame pointers add instructions without costing wall clock on this machine — the 9950X absorbs the extra register pressure. RSS is flat (+0.3%). If you want frame pointers for profiling, this sweep says take them.
- **`liq_video_frames_pool` gets 39% faster from `-fp` alone, with instruction count unchanged (+0.02%).** 4.94s → 3.00s, CV 0.2%/0.7% — it reproduces cleanly across all 5 invocations. Identical instructions and identical RSS with a 39% wall drop means this is a code-layout/alignment effect, not less work. `-fp-flambda` shows the same -40%. Worth an issue on its own: something in the stock 5.5.0 layout of this loop is pathological and frame pointers happen to perturb it away.
- **`-flambda`'s biggest wins are the allocation-heavy interpreters.** `liq_parse_typecheck` -41.9% wall (instr -30.3%), `owl_gc` -33.8% (instr -43.7%), `alt_ergo_yyll` -12.7%, `menhir_sql_parser` -12.0% (and -13.4% RSS), `ocamlformat_rocq` -12.0%.
- **The RSS cost of `-flambda` is concentrated, not spread.** Geomean +6.8% is driven by a few benches: `frama_c_eva_t` +88.8% (92 MB → 173 MB), `devkit_htmlstream` +39.1% (358 MB → 496 MB with `fp-flambda`), `ocamlformat_rocq` +12.4%, `alt_ergo_unsat_smt2` +12.3%. Note `frama_c_eva_t` runs in 0.39s — at that duration RSS is dominated by fixed startup cost, so treat the +89% as "flambda's constant is bigger here", not a leak.
- **Nothing regresses meaningfully on wall time.** The worst `flambda` wall regression across 31 benches is `pplacer_testsuite` +3.2%; the worst `fp` regression is `sedlex_tokenize` +4.3%, then `devkit_htmlstream` +4.0% and `devkit_network` +3.6% — all three are `fp`-specific instruction-count costs (+7.5%, +7.1%, +5.5%) that do show up as wall time. `coqc_corelib_stress` is inert across all four variants (±0.5%), as expected for a bench that spends its time in the Coq kernel.
- **One data-quality note:** `liq_video_frames_pool` under `fp-flambda` has one outlier invocation (3.87s against a 2.94–2.96s cluster, CV 13.1%). The median is robust; no other cell in the sweep exceeds 2% CV.

## Caveats

- Governor was `performance` for this run. The 2026-08-18 PR-14571 sweep in `results/latest` ran under `powersave` — **absolute wall times are not comparable across those two result sets**, only within-sweep deltas are.
- 31 benches, not the full 38 — this run predates the alt-ergo/coq/liq `_default`/`_large` rung split used by the 08-18 sweep.
