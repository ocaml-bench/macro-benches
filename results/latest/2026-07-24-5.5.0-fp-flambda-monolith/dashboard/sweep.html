Parameter sweeps

Heatmap of a metric across two swept dimensions (GC parameters). Only meaningful for runs that swept parameters (e.g. s × o); for single-config runs there are no dimensions to plot.

When two runtimes are compared, the grids above show each runtime's absolute values; below is their difference across the sweep — for every parameter point, how much the variant differs from the baseline. Negative (green) means the variant is better.
